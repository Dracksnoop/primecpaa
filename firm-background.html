About

Get to Know Prime CPA LLC

Prime CPA LLC is located in Alpharetta, Georgia and is an expert in all areas of accounting, taxes, and business services.

Working With Us

Strategic Guidance for Alpharetta’s Most Driven Professionals

In Alpharetta, Georgia, Prime CPA LLC supports the entrepreneurs, professionals, and high-net-worth individuals who want more than a standard accountant—they want a long-term partner who proactively strengthens their financial life. Through tax planning, tax preparation, bookkeeping, accounting, payroll, and full-service support for small to mid-sized businesses, the firm provides clarity, efficiency, and strategy for clients who expect precision at every level.

Who We Serve

Our practice is built for clients who value insight as much as accuracy, including:

Medical practices & physician groupsLaw officesRetail businessesCar dealershipsRestaurants & hospitality groupsReal estate professionals and investorsHigh-net-worth individuals

These are people who manage complex financial decisions daily—and want to understand the why behind the numbers.

Education as a Core Value

Founder Kumar Bhandari’s background shapes everything we do. As a CPA, Personal Financial Specialist (PFS), and holder of a Master’s in Taxation, Kumar brings rigorous technical expertise to every engagement. But he also brings something rarer: the heart of a teacher. He has spent years lecturing at the university level during the tax off-season, and that passion for education is woven into our client experience.

Clients who come with questions feel encouraged—not rushed. Curious minds who want to understand tax implications, financial habits, or business performance find a partner who welcomes thoughtful discussion.

A Modern, Upgraded Engagement Model

For those who want proactive strategy, the firm offers an annual tax-planning engagement designed for true partnership. Clients pay a fixed monthly fee for 12 months and receive:

01

Quarterly—or even monthly—tax strategy meetings

02

Unlimited appointments related to tax questions and decisions

03

Implementation of advanced tax-saving strategies

04

Preparation of their prior-year tax return (e.g., engagement in 2026 includes the 2025 return)

This model gives business owners and high-income individuals year-round access to a tax specialist—not just seasonal help.

For clients who simply need accurate, timely filings, the firm continues to offer traditional tax preparation as well.

Linking Tax Strategy to Wealth Strategy

With the PFS designation, Kumar is uniquely positioned at the intersection of tax and personal finance. While the firm does not sell investments, Kumar and his team help clients connect with vetted professionals who can deliver investment support aligned with tax-efficient goals. Every recommendation comes from a place of fiduciary responsibility, clarity, and long-term benefit.

Why People Choose Kumar and His Team

Clients come to the firm for:

  • Peace of mind and complete compliance
  • A more insightful, strategic view of their business and personal finances
  • Identifying tax-saving opportunities, both short- and long-term
  • Support in operating more efficiently within their existing financial structure
  • Genuine education and guidance—not just transactions

Kumar also maintains deep local involvement as Treasurer of NASEA and an active contributor to the Alpharetta professional community.
